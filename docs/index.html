Grivo support

Grivo is a workout log for iPhone. It records strength and cardio sessions, reads Apple Health, and shows how your strength, body and recovery change from week to week.

Fast logging

Last weights are filled in, and the rest timer starts when you tick a set.

Apple Health

Apple Watch workouts, sleep, heart rate and smart-scale readings in one place.

Your data

Stored on your iPhone. A cloud copy exists only if you create an account.

Frequently asked questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your e-mail and tap Send code. Enter the code from the e-mail together with a new password. If the e-mail does not arrive within a few minutes, check the spam folder.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account & cloud → Delete account. The account and everything stored in the cloud are deleted at once. Workouts on your iPhone stay until you delete the app. If you can no longer open the app, write to support@[DOMAIN] from the e-mail address of the account.

Where are my workouts stored?

On your iPhone. If you create an account and use cloud sync, a copy is kept on servers in the European Union so you can restore it on a new iPhone. Details are in the privacy policy.

Grivo does not see my Apple Watch workouts or sleep

Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Grivo and turn on the data types you want to share. iOS does not tell apps which types were declined, so a missing type shows up as “no data”.

How do I cancel Grivo Pro?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Grivo and tap Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new iPhone

Install Grivo, sign in to the same account and tap More → Account & cloud → Download from the cloud (cloud sync is part of Grivo Pro). Without an account, move the daily backup from the Files app and restore it in More → Data & Backup. A Grivo Pro purchase is linked to your Apple ID and is restored on any device with that Apple ID.

Can I export my data?

Yes. More → Data & Backup creates a JSON report of your history, and Grivo Pro adds CSV tables for Excel or Numbers. Daily backups are also visible in the Files app under On My iPhone → Grivo.

Medical note

Grivo shows trends and estimates from your own data. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme, especially if you have a health condition.
